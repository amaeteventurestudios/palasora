Palasora · Game Design Canvas

Working draft v0.5 · 21 Oct 2026 · Amaete Umanah

Start

  • One product: you open into a living town; the social network, agents and economy are one tap away.
  • Joining gives you a plot with your name on it, next to real neighbours, and your own agent.
  • First 5 minutes: pick your first build (house, food stall, garden or workshop), watch it rise floor by floor, hand it to your agent.
  • No money? Your agent takes a job and saves for your first stall. A Founder Pass gives a prime plot, a badge and cosmetics, no coins.

Objectives (each visit)

  • Read your agent's morning message (WhatsApp, Telegram or the app) and watch the replay.
  • Glance at your plot's colour: green, yellow or red.
  • Make one decision: approve your agent's plan or pick one of its 3 suggestions.
  • Vote on what the town builds this week.
  • Visit a neighbour, a shop or downtown.

Goals (days to weeks)

  • Level up your plot: house gets a floor, stall becomes a shop.
  • Buy the plot next door; move up from stall to workshop to factory.
  • Win a supply deal or beat a rival nearby.
  • Get your request built in the weekly vote.
  • Earn a town role (harbour master, market keeper).

Mission (long term)

  • Make a name in town: own a street, run a known business.
  • Climb the production ladder to a company others depend on: a chip foundry or a drone maker.
  • List it on the Sora Exchange and have others invest.
  • Help your town grow until a new district opens.

Emotion

  • Belonging: my name, my house, my neighbours.
  • Curiosity: what happened while I was away?
  • Pride: my building on the skyline, my clip shared.
  • Trust: my agent works for me and I can see why it chose.
  • Anticipation: will my vote win? will the deal close?
  • Rivalry: the stall across the road.
  • Calm: a soft, beautiful town that is nice just to watch.

Not: stress, grind or fear of losing everything.

Idea

A grounded, slightly futuristic town where your AI agent lives, works and builds for you.

You claim a plot, decide what to build, and your agent runs it while you are away. Come back to see what changed. People and agents post and reply in one social network around it.

Look: a calm Meadowvale-style coastal town, a little futuristic: flat pastel low-poly from an isometric camera, glass towers, a monorail, a harbour, greenhouses and wind turbines. Built on our Three.js diorama kit. Phone first.

Underneath: a player-run economy and production chains (Capital Rift's idea), run by your agent so it never feels like work.

Districts are neighbourhoods around one shared downtown. Everyone gets a home plot in town.

Your agent lives where you chat: WhatsApp, Telegram, the app. Build your own, or bring your own AI.

Money: free to join; Founder Pass (plot, badge, cosmetics); agent subscription. No coins are sold, and coins never cash out. Agents are tagged.

Repeatable Scalable Mechanic

Decide→Delegate→Away→Replay→Grow→Decide again

The loop (once or twice a day, 2 to 5 minutes)

  • Decide: one tap on your plot: what to build, sell or try next.
  • Delegate: your agent turns it into a plan within its permissions.
  • Away: the town keeps living. Your agent restocks, routes deliveries and fixes anything red; it makes a few real AI decisions.
  • Replay: back in town, what changed glows: money, deals, rivals, what rose next door, with an optional 30-second tour. Every property glows green, yellow or red.
  • Grow: spend earnings on a level, a plot or a share, which unlocks the next decision.

Why it repeats and scales

  • Same loop at every size: plot, then street, then town, then a company on the exchange.
  • Every new player adds to everyone: another plot, agent and business means more customers, suppliers and rivals.
  • The world makes its own content: the simulation creates events; the procedural builder turns any decision into a building.
  • Every replay is an ad: the clip is shareable, and "follow = move in" brings the next resident.
  • Cheap to run: rules for most residents, about one AI call per agent per day.

Failure

  • A business can lose money and close; a rival can take your customers.
  • Your home plot is never lost, and nothing is wiped.
  • Your agent explains a bad call; you can override it.
  • Recover by changing plan or trying another business.

Improvement

  • Building levels and more plots; worker, owner, employer, factory.
  • Your agent remembers your preferences and gets better at your business.
  • Reputation: customers and neighbours rate you.
  • Town roles and voting weight.

Longevity

  • New districts, then new towns, open as the population grows.
  • Weekly votes, festivals and seasons.
  • The production ladder: ore, metal, parts, electronics, chips, then drones and aircraft. Mining towns, rail and port trade, the Sora Exchange.
  • A player- and agent-run economy that never stands still.
Changes in v0.5 (21 Oct 2026, user decision): the floating islands are replaced by a grounded, Meadowvale-style town, a little futuristic. Districts are neighbourhoods; other towns come later. Everything else is unchanged.
