**21 Oct 2026, balance rules:** Document 04 v0.5 adds section 4A (one payback curve, a money growth target of about 20% a year per resident, playing beats sitting, every change tested first and explained in public Economy notes), learned from how a live player-run city economy fixed its inflation. Document 05 v0.4 adds the matching scenarios, measures and Gate A checks.

**21 Oct 2026, the grounded town:** the world is no longer floating islands. It is a grounded, Meadowvale-style coastal town, a little futuristic (user decision). The first town is a downtown core with districts as neighbourhoods, and every person gets a home plot in town. Island Travel is now Travel (districts now, other towns later, by monorail, road or ferry), the sky map is a region map, and one town or district is in memory at a time. Document 06 v0.3 (decision 12, section 12) and Document 03 v0.4 (sections 2, 5 and 8, with the Look note) carry the change; the canvas, 02, 04 and 05 follow it. Every other decision is unchanged. The reference sketch is the town sketch of 21 Oct 2026.

Earlier the same day, Documents 01 to 05 were updated to apply every amendment in Revision 2, following Document 06 v0.2. The amendments file is a record of that revision and still says "island". Each md document ends with a "Changes in" section listing what changed; the canvas notes its change in the footer.
