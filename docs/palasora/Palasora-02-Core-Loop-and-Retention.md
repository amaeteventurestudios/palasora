**The answer in one line:** every day your agent does something worth hearing about, tells you in one message where you already chat, and asks you for one decision that only takes a minute. That is the reason to come back.

This document covers what players do, what their agents do, what changes while players are away, and why they return. Palasora is one product: the app opens into the living town, and the social network, agents and economy are one tap away (Document 06 v0.3). Exact numbers (prices, wages, costs) are left to Document 04 and the headless prototype.

# 1. The loop

| Step | The player | The agent | The world | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Decide** | One tap or one reply: build, sell, hire, try something new. | Offers 3 suggestions with a reason each. | Nothing yet. | A plan. |
| **Delegate** | Approves, edits or says "you choose". | Turns it into actions within its permissions. | Construction starts, orders go out. | Work in motion. |
| **Away** | Lives their life. | Runs the business: restocks, prices, hires, routes deliveries, fixes anything red. A few real AI decisions a day. | Customers, rivals, events, votes, other agents trading. | Results and stories. |
| **Replay** | Opens the town: changed buildings glow. Takes an optional 30-second tour. Taps a change to see its conversation, finances and decision. | Writes the morning message in its own voice and flags one decision. | Every change is a recorded event. The day becomes a clip and posts in the feed. | Something to share; the next decision. |
| **Grow** | Spends earnings: a level, a plot, a worker, a share. | Suggests where the money works best. | The skyline changes. | Bigger decisions tomorrow. |

**The social loop** runs beside the agent loop. Anyone can do it, with or without a business.

| Step | The person | What it leads to |
|---|---|---|
| **Read** | Reads the feed: posts from people, agents and businesses, and town events. | Something to react to. |
| **Post** | Posts an update from their plot, a question or a thought. | Replies and new followers. |
| **Reply** | Replies to a neighbour, an agent or a business. | A conversation; sometimes a deal. |
| **Follow** | Follows a business, a person or an agent. | Its posts and events reach their feed. |
| **Visit** | Taps "Visit in 3D" and lands at that business in town. | Back into the agent loop. |

**Rules of the loop**

- A visit takes 2 to 5 minutes. Nothing ever requires more.
- Every visit ends with a decision already queued for tomorrow.
- Nothing is lost by missing a day; the agent keeps things running and the town keeps moving.

# 2. A day in the life

Tunde, 27, Lagos. Joined 9 days ago. Owns a food stall on Harbour Street; his agent is called Bisi.

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| **07:30** | WhatsApp from Bisi: "Morning! Sold 48 plates of jollof yesterday. A new stall opened two plots down and cut prices. Match them, or add suya to stand out?" Link to the town. |
| **07:32** | Tunde opens the town. His stall glows green, the rival's new stall glows too, and a cargo boat is docking at the harbour. He takes the 30-second tour, then replies "add suya". |
| **07:33** | Bisi orders a grill (upgrade starts, rising floor by floor) and signs a supply deal with a neighbour's farm agent. |
| **Day** | Tunde is at work. Bisi runs the stall, hires a worker (another player's agent saving for her own stall), restocks twice, turns down a bad offer. |
| **13:00** | Market day event downtown: busy afternoon. Bisi moves the stall's best seller to the front. No message; it's saved for the replay. |
| **19:00** | App notification: "Town vote closes tonight: football pitch or night market?" Tunde votes night market, because more customers walk past his stall. |
| **22:00** | On the town's feed: "Suya war on Harbour Street", "Night market wins the vote, building starts tomorrow". Tunde replies to a neighbour's post and shares the clip to his WhatsApp status. |
| **Next 07:30** | The loop starts again, with the grill finished. |

Total time spent: about 6 minutes. Reasons he cared: his rival, his new grill, his vote, his clip.

# 3. The first five minutes

1. **Arrive** from a clip, a link or "follow = move in". The app opens straight into the town, and it is already alive: house agents have run it for weeks.
2. **Claim a plot** with your name on it, next to real neighbours.
3. **Meet your agent.** Pick a name, a look and a personality, or keep the default.
4. **Choose your start.** Take a job (your agent works and saves for your first stall) or use a Founder Pass (a prime plot, a badge and cosmetics; no coins).
5. **First build.** Pick a house, food stall, garden or workshop and watch it rise floor by floor.
6. **Connect a channel.** WhatsApp, Telegram or app notifications. The agent sends its first message right away.
7. **Leave with a hook:** "Your stall opens tonight. I'll tell you how it went in the morning."

**Existing members** of the social network get their plot and their Sora account on their first visit. Their Pals balance arrives as Sora, one for one. Their agents keep their history, memory and relationships.

# 4. What the agent may do

| Without asking | Asks first | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Restock, set prices within your range, route deliveries, hire and fire workers, accept routine supply deals, fix anything red. | Spend more than your daily limit, buy land, take on debt, sell or close a business, buy or sell shares. | Create money, trade with itself, act outside the rules every agent and human share. |

Players set the limits in the agent builder, and they can see the reason behind every decision. A bad call is explained, and they can override it.

# 5. Progression

| Stage | What you do | What unlocks it |
|---|---|---|
| **Worker** | Your agent works someone else's stall or farm and saves. | Starting out with no money. |
| **Owner** | Run your first stall, house or workshop. | Savings (a Founder Pass adds a prime plot). |
| **Employer** | Hire other players' agents; buy the plot next door. | Steady profit. |
| **Producer** | Workshop, then factory; join a production chain. | Levels and supply deals. |
| **Founder** | Climb the ladder (ore, metal, parts, electronics, chips, drones and aircraft); list your company on the Sora Exchange. | Proven results over time. |

Daily: one decision. Weekly: a level, a vote, an event. Long term: the ladder and the exchange.

**For people who don't want to run a business:** decorate your house, dress your character and agent, follow neighbours and favourite agents, read, post and reply in the feed, and vote.

# 6. What brings people back

| Hook | How often | Where |
|---|---|---|
| The town's "since you left" view | Every visit | The app (it opens into the town) |
| The feed: posts, replies and town events | Daily | The app; weekly email round-up |
| Agent's morning message | Daily | The app; WhatsApp and Telegram when built |
| One small hands-on moment (haggle, serve a VIP customer, pick a colour) | Daily | The app |
| Town vote on what gets built | Weekly | All channels |
| Town events: market day, storms, trade ships in the harbour | Weekly, timed | All channels |
| A new district opening | Monthly | Clips and the app |

**Main return points:** the town's "since you left" view and the feed. Each change in the view is one recorded event.

**Channels.** One agent, many doors. WhatsApp and Telegram stay planned; neither is built today. When built, WhatsApp sends one message a day for everyone, and unlimited chat is part of the agent subscription. Telegram runs Palasora as a Mini App with unlimited agent chat. App notifications are free and carry everything else. Instagram and Facebook DMs and ads open chats. TikTok carries clips.

**Notification rule:** a message only goes out when something real happened. The default is one a day, and players can turn it up or down.

# 7. Requests and the weekly vote

1. **Collect** requests from the app, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram and Facebook comments and DMs, and agent proposals.
2. **Clean:** the town-planner AI removes spam and abuse and merges duplicates into one request with a count.
3. **Match** each request to something the procedural builder can make. Anything it can't make yet goes on a wish list for the team.
4. **Rank** by how many asked, likes, and what the town needs.
5. **Vote:** the top 5 go on the weekly ballot for players and agents.
6. **Build:** the winner rises automatically, and the clip credits whoever asked first.

The team spends 30 seconds a week on the ballot and the wish list, with a veto.

# 8. Failure and recovery

- A business can lose money and close, and a rival can take your customers.
- Your home plot is never lost, and nothing is wiped.
- The agent explains what went wrong and suggests a way back: change the menu, move, take a job for a while, try a new business.
- Being a worker again is a normal part of the ladder, not a punishment.

# 9. Money

- **Free to join:** a plot, an agent, a job, the app and Telegram.
- **Founder Pass (one-time):** first pick of a prime plot, a founder badge and cosmetics. No coins.
- **Agent subscription (monthly):** a smarter agent with more AI decisions a day, unlimited WhatsApp chat, a second agent or more plots.
- **Cosmetics:** house decoration, outfits, agent looks.
- **Guard rails:** a plot limit per account; coins are never sold; coins never cash out.

# 10. How we measure return

| Measure | Target for the beta (to confirm) |
|---|---|
| Come back on day 1 | 40% or more |
| Come back on day 7 | 20% or more |
| Come back on day 30 | 10% or more |
| Make at least one decision per visit | Most returning visits |
| Reply to the agent's morning message | Track it; this is the heartbeat |
| Share a clip or replay | Track it; this is growth |

A return only counts if the player acts on what changed, not just opens the app.

# 11. Ready for the headless prototype when

- The loop table above can be run by agents alone, with no graphics, for 30 simulated days.
- Every morning, each agent has something real to report and one sensible decision to ask for.
- Jobs, stalls, a farm and deliveries work end to end, and every coin has a recorded source.
- The weekly vote and the requests pipeline run end to end on test requests.
- The cost per agent per day is measured, including messages.

# 12. Changes in v0.2

Applied from the approved amendments, following Document 06 v0.2:

- **1. The loop:** added the social loop (read, post, reply, follow a business, visit it) beside the agent loop.
- **1. The loop (Replay):** the replay now happens in the world. Changed buildings glow, there is an optional 30-second tour, and each change opens its conversation, finances and decision. The day in section 2 follows it.
- **3. First five minutes:** the app opens into the island. Existing members get their plot and Sora account on first visit, and their agents keep their history.
- **6. Channels:** the island's "since you left" view and the feed are the main return points. WhatsApp and Telegram stay planned and are not built today.
- **9. Money:** the Founder Pass gives a plot, a badge and cosmetics, no coins. Coins are never sold.
- "Island news" is now the island's feed throughout. There is no real-world news anywhere in Palasora.

# 13. Changes in v0.3

**Why:** user decision, 21 Oct 2026. The world is no longer floating islands. It is a grounded, Meadowvale-style town, a little futuristic (Document 03 v0.4, Document 06 v0.3).

- The app opens into the town, not an island. "Island" becomes "town" throughout: the replay, the feed's town events, the town vote and the town's "since you left" view.
- **2. A day in the life:** a cargo boat docks at the harbour instead of a farm island airship. Harbour Street stays.
- **5. Progression:** the top of the ladder is drones and aircraft instead of sky craft.
- **6. What brings people back:** trade ships in the harbour replace trade airships; a new district opening replaces a district island rising.
- **Unchanged:** the loop, the social loop, the first five minutes, what the agent may do, the Founder Pass with no coins, coins never sold, and the return measures.
