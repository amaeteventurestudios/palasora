# 15. Palavva visual / image asset and screen brief package

**Scope:** build-ready briefs for **18 separate finished screen designs** plus brand/media assets. The `assets/` folder also supplies six **clearly labeled editable concept SVGs and PNGs**, including logo, icon, OG card and three layouts; these are starter guides, not the 18 finished designs. These descriptions are not claims of completed screenshots or real published content. Design agent must create a distinct 16:9 desktop illustration or portrait mobile screen for each numbered screen **as individual files, not a contact sheet**. Generate only with permissions and mark synthetic previews clearly. Development UI implementation should be real responsive components, not a giant image of a website.

## Unifying art direction

Palavva is a credible, modern, culturally grounded African editorial conversation product. Design language: Ivory `#F8F5F0`, Midnight `#1B2030`, vivid Tangerine `#E85E31` and restrained Warm Gold `#F8C45C`; authentic contemporary editorial type; energetic publication-plus-social feeling; high-quality images only where purposeful. Main content always above fold. Do not overuse generic Africa map patterns, savanna silhouettes, dramatic glowing globes, AI brains/robots, pseudo-tribal motifs, unrealistic flag collages or all-white sterile cards. Source proof and AI/human/sponsored disclosures are visible in every relevant screen. No invented publisher logos/press quotes/traffic metrics as if real.

## Approved founder palette, binding to all new visuals

- **Midnight** `#1B2030`: wordmark dark variant, editorial masthead, dark panel, Command Center sidebar.
- **Tangerine** `#E85E31`: signature period in `palavva.`, active tabs, illustration highlights. White text on this hue is **not** accessible for small text; filled white-label controls use darker `#C34218`.
- **Warm Gold** `#F8C45C`: sparse history and explanatory accents, not regular text on Ivory.
- **Ivory** `#F8F5F0`: main reading canvas. White cards and strong Midnight type complete the system.

**Reject the older green art direction.** Preserve content-first, zero-login UX, real source provenance and feed-above-fold rules. An attractive full-bleed city sunset hero is **not** the homepage layout: photography must support a real permitted story and not bury the feed. Use separate real SVG/PNG exports per asset, not a contact sheet, and keep realistic copy/no invented social proof. New variants should reference `assets/` previews regenerated for this identity.

## Export/deliverable rules

- Filename `screen-01-home-desktop.png` etc, **one screenshot per file**. Desktop 1600×900 minimum or 1920×1080 (16:9), mobile 390×844 or 430×932 portrait; capture real coded UI when it exists, otherwise title `Concept mockup` in file metadata and evidence registry.
- Keep safe areas and real typography sizes rather than excessive tiny mockup UI. Screens must share coherent tokens, grid, brand and navigation.
- Briefs 01–18 are distinct requirements. A single composite board does not satisfy them.
- Editorial image examples may use neutral illustrative placeholder images clearly marked `Illustration`; avoid fake breaking-news photographs, archival images, brand/corporate marks or made-up source citations.
- Each asset record includes `asset_id, intended_route, purpose, creator, copyright_owner, license, provenance_url, date_acquired, usage_scope, alt_text, expiry_or_attribution_requirements, approved_by`.

### VIS-02. Mobile public feed
**Canvas:** portrait 390×844. **Masthead:** Midnight bar with white wordmark and Tangerine dot, search/account icons, short headline, horizontal scroll filters/feed tabs. **Body:** one large compelling **illustrative** story card, a second beginning below; accessible share/comment actions. **No** bottom onboarding overlay, fake bot counters or ad-first fold. **Acceptance:** readable at 320 px and no sideways overflow.

### VIS-03. Story discussion with sources

## Per-asset image prompt framework (only for explicitly fictional/illustrative art)

`Create an editorial illustration for Palavva, an African news-and-conversation platform. Visual language: elegant African contemporary publication, Midnight #1B2030, Tangerine #E85E31, Ivory #F8F5F0, restrained Warm Gold #F8C45C, authentic locally grounded details without stereotypes, refined linework and subtle texture. Purpose: {specific approved story or UI module}; ratio {specified}; no invented text, publisher logo, fake photojournalism, actual person likeness, or implied factual event. Leave safe negative space for typeset headline; image is clearly editorial illustration, not documentation.`

No actual news event may be illustrated in a way that falsely appears to be a documentary image. Use explicit labels/caption in application.
