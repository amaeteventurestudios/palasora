## About Palavva

# About Palavva

**Africa has a lot to talk about.**

Every day, something remarkable happens across Africa. A business launches. A government changes a policy. A musician opens a new door. Someone builds a better way to solve an everyday problem. An old decision starts shaping a new conversation.

But a headline rarely tells the whole story. Who is affected? What led here? What happened when a similar idea was tried before? What can we learn from the people doing the work?

That's why we're building Palavva. We bring together fresh news, business, technology, history, culture, and thoughtful conversation. Our editorial AI helps explain sources, ask useful questions, and make connections. People bring their experience, disagreement, insight, and curiosity.

You might learn why a small food business struggles to reach supermarket shelves. You might discover an inventor changing an industry. Or you might see how a decision made decades ago still matters today.

We also believe African founders, creators, and small businesses deserve a chance to be discovered without needing a huge publicity budget. Discovery should come from genuine interest, clear information, and honest discussion, not bought praise.

We won't turn every story into an argument. We won't fill your feed with bots congratulating one another. And we won't trade truth or human dignity for a few more clicks.

You don't need an account to read or share. Come in, find something interesting, ask a better question, and tell somebody about it.

**Welcome to Palavva. The conversation is just getting started.**

## Editorial Principles

**H1:** `Curiosity deserves the truth.`

A useful conversation starts with the facts. We link to original sources when available, explain what is confirmed, and distinguish interpretation from evidence. Our goal isn't to keep people angry or make every issue a contest. It's to help people understand what happened and why it matters.

We respect the dignity of people in our stories. We cover difficult problems without turning someone's suffering into entertainment. We make room for builders, solutions, culture, and ordinary achievements as well as hard questions.

AI can help organize research and explore several perspectives, but it isn't a witness and it doesn't get to invent evidence. Humans remain responsible for editorial decisions, especially on sensitive subjects. Businesses can sponsor visible placements; they can't purchase our conclusions.

When we get something wrong, we want to correct it clearly. Use `Request a correction` on a story or contact us with the precise issue and a source that helps us check it.

**Links:** `How our AI works` · `View corrections` · `Report a concern`.

## AI Transparency

**H1:** `What AI does here, and what it doesn't.`

Palavva uses AI to help read permitted sources, group related stories, explore history, draft explanations, and bring useful questions into discussions. Its contributions are labeled `AI analysis` and identify the editorial role involved.

AI does not make an article true simply by agreeing with itself. We check evidence and use human review for sensitive or uncertain claims. AI-generated accounts are not presented as real people. A business representative, whether human or automated, must disclose whom it represents. You can read original sources, challenge a claim, and request a correction.

**Callout:** `See a claim that needs checking? Tell us what it is and where we can verify it.`
